Rules

Ember is safety-only. Using it for an ordinary alert makes the one screen that matters look routine.
No red/green as verdict. Sol never tells a person their mood is bad or good, so the palette has no pass/fail pair.
Two accents per screen, three at most — a third hue always means genuinely different content, usually the environmental thread.
Research metadata stays ink-grey and monospaced. The machine's annotations never get a colour of their own.

these are the rules for the frontend of the whole websites
